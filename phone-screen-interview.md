# How to Pass a Recruiter Phone Screen: A Practical Guide

The recruiter phone screen is usually 15 to 30 minutes long, and it decides whether you ever meet the hiring team. It is less about deep skills and more about fit, logistics and clear communication. Here is how to prepare so the call works in your favour.

## 1. Know what the recruiter is checking

Most recruiters have a short checklist: Are you genuinely interested in this role? Do your experience and the job description roughly match? Are your salary expectations, location and start date workable? Can you explain your background clearly? Prepare for those four things and you have covered most of the call.

## 2. Prepare a 60-second background summary

Write a short version of "tell me about yourself": current role, two achievements with numbers, and why this position is the logical next step. Say it out loud a few times so it sounds natural rather than memorised. Recruiters often pass your wording straight to the hiring manager, so make it easy to repeat.

## 3. Research the company for ten minutes

You do not need deep analysis. Read the job post again, the company's About page and one recent news item or product launch. Have one specific sentence ready about why the company interests you, such as a product you used or a problem they are solving.

## 4. Decide your salary range before the call

Recruiters frequently ask about compensation in the first call. Look up market data for the role and city, pick a range whose bottom you would be happy with, and practise saying it calmly. If you prefer not to share a number yet, ask what range is budgeted for the role.

## 5. Set up for a phone or video call

Use a quiet room, a charged phone or headset and a stable connection. Keep your resume, the job description and a few notes in front of you. Standing up while you talk can make your voice sound more energetic.

## 6. Ask two good questions

End with questions that help you and show interest: "What does success look like in the first six months?" and "What are the next steps and timeline?" Avoid asking about perks before you have an offer.

## 7. Follow up the same day

Send a short thank-you email within a few hours: thank them for their time, restate one reason you are excited about the role, and confirm any details they asked for.

If you get nervous in live calls, an AI assistant can help you rehearse or keep you on track.
